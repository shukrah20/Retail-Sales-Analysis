# Retail Sales Analysis


## Objective

To show how sales, profit and orders vary across brands, product colours, income levels, customer gender and months, using an interactive dashboard that can be filtered by region and category.

## Overview

This report presents an analysis of sales performance across brands, regions, customer demographics and product categories for Q1 2023 (January to March). A total of 250 customers generated 15,657 orders, producing a total revenue of £3,106,350, with a total cost of £2,174,445 and a total profit of £931,905.

**Tools used:** Power BI Desktop, Power query, DAX measures, slicers and interactive visuals

## Key Metrics

| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | 250 |
| Total Orders | 15,657 |
| Total Revenue | £3,106,350 |
| Total Cost | £2,174,445 |
| Total Profit | £931,905 |

## Key Findings

**Brand performance:** Apple emerged as the most profitable brand with £196,890 in profit, followed by Lenovo (£160,140) and Samsung (£159,510). HP, Asus and Dell recorded lower profits, with Dell lowest at £125,850. This is total profit, so it reflects sales volume and price as well as brand strength.

**Monthly orders:** Order volume was steady across the quarter, with 5,180 orders in February, 5,236 in January and 5,241 in March. The differences between months are small, so the data shows no clear trend over Q1.

**Color preference:** White was the most purchased product color, followed by Gray and then Black. This could help guide future inventory and product selection decisions.

**Income level and profit:** Medium income customers contributed the most to overall profit (£346,920), followed by High income (£302,205) and Low income (£282,780) customers. The three groups are fairly close, so marketing could be tested across all of them, with medium income customers as a reasonable starting point.

**Revenue by gender:** Female customers generated £2,077,700 (67% of total revenue) and male customers generated £1,028,650 (33%).

**Customer demographics:** The customer base consists of 250 customers spread across four regions (East, South, West and North). The age range of customers falls between 25 and 40 years, with an average age of 33, indicating a predominantly young adult customer base.

## Dashboard

The dashboard combines five KPI cards with five visuals (quantity purchased by color, revenue by customer gender, income level by profit, profit by brand and monthly orders). Region and Category slicers and a Clear all slicers button that let the viewer filter every visual at once.

## Recommendations

- **Focus on Apple and Lenovo:** these brands drive the most profit and should be prioritized in stock and marketing.
- **Keep White and Gray in stock:** given their popularity, strong availability in these colors could drive more sales.
- **Review the gender mix:** female customers generate about two thirds of revenue, so check whether this comes from more customers or higher spend before targeting campaigns.
- **Monitor monthly orders:** volume was steady in Q1, so track it over a longer period before planning promotions.
- **Regional strategy:** use the Region slicer to find the region that generates the most revenue and allocate more marketing budget there.
